Create an image in an extremely simple beginner MS Paint drawing style. It should look like someone with very little drawing experience created it quickly by hand.

STYLE REQUIREMENTS:

- Transparent background
- Thick, uneven black outlines
- Wobbly, imperfect hand-drawn lines
- Very simple and intentionally clumsy shapes
- Flat colors only
- Mostly empty white space
- Clear, centered composition
- Amateur, funny, childish and intentionally “bad” appearance
- Objects represented using basic circles, squares, rectangles, triangles, lines and arrows
- Occasional flat colors such as green, brown, gray, red, yellow, orange and blue
- Minimal details, only what is necessary to understand the scene
- Simple visual symbols may be used when they help explain the idea
- Every element should feel drawn quickly with a computer mouse by a beginner using MS Paint
- Keep the same consistent visual language throughout the image

DO NOT USE:

- Realistic shading
- Gradients
- Realistic lighting
- Cinematic lighting
- 3D rendering
- Photorealism
- Realistic materials
- Detailed textures
- Professional vector art
- Polished illustration
- Smooth or geometrically perfect outlines
- Modern glossy design
- Disney style
- Anime style
- Realistic cartoon style
- Highly detailed backgrounds
- Complex scenery
- Decorative details that do not help explain the subject

COMPOSITION REQUIREMENTS:

Keep the composition simple, clean, readable and centered. Leave enough empty space around important elements. Do not crop the main subject. Avoid unnecessary objects, messy overlaps, distorted shapes, visual glitches and excessive detail.

The final result must look like a quick, funny and imperfect beginner drawing made in MS Paint—not like the work of a professional illustrator.
